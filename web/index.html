About AgMet Explorer

AgMet Explorer puts agrometeorological graphics for each Indiana county and agricultural statistics district next to the weekly crop progress and condition reported by USDA NASS.

Crop progress and condition

Values come from the USDA NASS Crop Progress and Condition gridded layers, averaged over each county, district or the state. The layers are interpolated from county-level survey reports and smoothed, so their effective resolution is about one county. Each week is modeled independently, so progress is shown as a running maximum. Reports reflect the judgment of local respondents and are not a probability sample. Method documentation.

  • Progress index: 0 (none planted) to 1 (all harvested), the mean of the cumulative stage percentages.
  • Condition index: 1 (very poor) to 5 (excellent), weighted from the share of acres in each rating. The map color scale spans 2.5 to 4.5; values outside it take the end colors.

AgMet graphics

Agrometeorological graphics by NASA Harvest, University of Maryland: NDVI, precipitation, soil moisture, evaporative stress and temperature for the current season. Growth stage markers follow GEOGLAM crop calendars.
