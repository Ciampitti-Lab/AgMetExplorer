About the data

Crop progress and condition come from the USDA NASS Crop Progress and Condition gridded layers, averaged over each county or district. The layers are interpolated from county-level survey reports and smoothed, so their effective resolution is about one county. Each week is modeled independently, so progress is shown as a running maximum. Reports reflect the judgment of local respondents and are not a probability sample. Method documentation.

AgMet graphics

Agrometeorological graphics by NASA Harvest, University of Maryland. Growth stage markers follow GEOGLAM crop calendars.
